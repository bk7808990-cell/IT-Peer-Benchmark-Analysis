# SQL Analysis: Business Insights Summary

After running the 11 SQL queries on our 4-year peer dataset, here are the key takeaways for Persistent Systems.

## 1. Growth & Scale
- **Growth Leaders:** KPIT (30.2% 4-Year CAGR) and Persistent (29.9% 4-Year CAGR) have grown the fastest. Zensar (6.9%) and Mphasis (10.0%) lagged behind.
- **Market Position:** By FY25 revenue, Mphasis is the largest in this group (₹14,229 Cr), followed by Coforge (₹12,073 Cr). Persistent sits at 3rd place (₹11,938 Cr).
- **Recent Momentum:** In FY25, Coforge had the highest YoY growth (34%), while Persistent grew by a solid 21.5%.

## 2. Profitability (EBITDA Margin)
- **Margin Stability:** Persistent has maintained a steady EBITDA margin of around 17-18% for the last 5 years.
- **Peer Comparison:** In FY25, Persistent's margin was 17.24%. KPIT (21.04%) and Mphasis (18.60%) are more profitable. The Peer Median (excluding Persistent) is 18.47%.

## 3. Operational Efficiency
- **Employee Cost Ratio:** Persistent spends 57.61% of its revenue on employees. Zensar (64.20%) and KPIT (63.32%) spend more, while Cyient (50.33%) is the most efficient.
- **Revenue per Employee (FY25):** Persistent generates ₹48.54 Lakhs per employee. It ranks **2nd** in the peer group, behind Zensar (₹50.29 Lakhs) and ahead of Cyient (₹46.59 Lakhs).

## 4. Financial Impact & Gap (vs Peer Median)
- **The Margin Gap:** Persistent's margin (17.24%) is 123 basis points (bps) lower than the Peer Median (18.47%). This represents a direct opportunity loss of roughly **₹146 Cr in EBITDA**.
- **Sensitivity:** If we reduce employee costs by 5% (approx ₹344 Cr), the EBITDA margin will jump from 17.24% to **20.12%** (a 288 bps gain).

## 5. Overall Ranking
When combining Margin Rank and Revenue-per-Employee Rank for FY25, KPIT and Mphasis rank at the top, while Persistent ranks **3rd overall**.

# Peer Selection Memo

## 1. Objective
To ensure a fair and meaningful "apples-to-apples" comparison, Persistent Systems was benchmarked against a carefully selected set of 6 mid-tier Indian IT services peers. Comparing Persistent with Tier-1 giants (like TCS, Infosys, or Wipro) would be misleading due to massive differences in scale, revenue, and operational structures.

## 2. Selection Criteria
The peer group was selected based on three strict quantitative and qualitative filters:

1.  **Revenue Band:** Companies within ±50% of Persistent Systems' FY25 revenue (approx ₹2,000 Cr to ₹15,000 Cr). This ensures similar scale.
2.  **Geography & Listing:** India-based, publicly listed companies to ensure data reliability and regulatory consistency.
3.  **Service Mix:** IT services companies with a focus on digital transformation, enterprise services, or engineering R&D.

## 3. Selected Peer Set (6 Companies)

| Company | HQ | Business Focus | Rationale for Selection |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **KPIT Technologies** | Pune | Automotive software (EV, Autonomous) | Niche specialization provides a high-margin benchmark. |
| **Coforge** | Noida | BFSI, Travel | High-growth trajectory, similar mid-tier dynamics. |
| **Mphasis** | Bengaluru | BFSI, Insurance, Cloud | Similar operational scale and large-cap mid-tier structure. |
| **LTTS (L&T Tech)** | Bengaluru | Engineering R&D (ER&D) | Pure-play ER&D benchmark for technical talent costs. |
| **Cyient** | Hyderabad | Engineering, Geospatial | Similar ER&D focus, provides an efficiency baseline. |
| **Zensar** | Pune | Digital transformation, AI | Mid-tier IT services; provides a lower-end margin baseline. |

## 4. Excluded Companies & Why
- **Tier-1 Giants (TCS, Infosys, Wipro):** Out of scope due to 10x-20x higher revenue scale.
- **Product Companies (Zoho, Freshworks):** Different business model (SaaS vs. Services).
- **Small/Captive Centers:** Lack of reliable public financial data for a 5-year comparative analysis.

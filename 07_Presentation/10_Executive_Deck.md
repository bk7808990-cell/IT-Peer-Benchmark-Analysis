# Executive Presentation Deck: Persistent Systems
**Audience:** Business Leadership & Strategy Team
**Objective:** To benchmark Persistent Systems against 6 peers and recommend strategies to close the profitability gap.

## Slide 1: Title Slide
*   **Title:** IT Peer Benchmark & Strategic Gap Analysis
*   **Subtitle:** Moving from High Growth to Profitable Growth
*   **Date:** October 2026

## Slide 2: The Landscape (Peer Comparison)
**Headline:** We are growing fast, but leaving money on the table.
*   **Our Strength (Growth):** We have the 2nd highest 4-year Revenue CAGR of **29.9%** in our peer group.
*   **The Paradox (Profitability):** Despite high growth, our FY25 EBITDA margin is **17.24%**, placing us 5th out of 7 peers. 
*   **The Benchmark:** The peer median margin is **18.47%**. We are losing out on **123 basis points (bps)** of profit compared to the median.

## Slide 3: The Core Findings (Where is the leak?)
**Headline:** Our Operations costs are the main culprit, not just salaries.
*   **Finding 1 (The Surprise):** Our Employee Cost Ratio (57.61%) is actually better than the peer median (58.92%). Our salary costs are under control.
*   **Finding 2 (The Leak):** The margin gap is primarily driven by **Other Operating Costs**, which are dragging our margin down by **254 bps**.
*   **Finding 3 (The Strength):** We have a highly productive workforce, generating **₹48.54 Lakhs** in revenue per employee (2nd highest in the peer group).

## Slide 4: Strategic Recommendations
**Headline:** Three actionable steps to boost our margins.
1.  **Streamline Operations:** Implement Zero-Based Budgeting for admin and facility costs. *(Impact: Target to recover the 254 bps gap).*
2.  **Optimize Employee Cost (5% Cut):** Reduce bench strength and improve offshore utilization. *(Impact: Adds 288 bps to margin).*
3.  **Leverage Productivity:** Shift from chasing low-margin revenue to negotiating value-based pricing. *(Impact: Protects growth while improving profit).*

## Slide 5: Next Steps & Implementation
**Headline:** Pilot, Measure, and Scale.
*   **Immediate Action:** Run a 4-week pilot in the Delivery department to identify operating waste and test the 5% cost reduction strategy.
*   **Tracking:** Use the newly built Power BI dashboard to monitor the Margin Bridge monthly.
*   **Financial Goal:** Increase EBITDA margin from **17.24% to 19.5% - 20% by end of FY27**, generating an additional ₹500+ Cr in EBITDA as a strategic target.

# Executive Presentation Deck: Persistent Systems
**Audience:** Business Leadership & Strategy Team
**Objective:** To benchmark Persistent Systems against 6 peers and recommend strategies to close the profitability gap.

---

## Slide 1: Title Slide
*   **Title:** IT Peer Benchmark & Strategic Gap Analysis
*   **Subtitle:** Moving from High Growth to Profitable Growth
*   **Date:** October 2026

---

## Slide 2: The Landscape (Peer Comparison)
**Headline:** We are growing fast, but leaving money on the table.

*   **Our Strength (Growth):** We have the 2nd highest 5-year Revenue CAGR of **23.3%** in our peer group. The sales engine is working.
*   **The Paradox (Profitability):** Despite high growth, our FY25 EBITDA margin is **17.24%**, placing us 5th out of 7 peers. 
*   **The Benchmark:** The peer median margin is **18.37%**, and top-tier peers like KPIT (21.04%) are significantly ahead. We are losing out on 113 basis points (bps) of profit compared to the median.

---

## Slide 3: The Core Findings (Where is the leak?)
**Headline:** Our Operations costs are the main culprit, not just salaries.

*   **Finding 1 (The Surprise):** Our Employee Cost Ratio (57.61%) is actually better than the peer median (58.00%). Our salary costs are under control.
*   **Finding 2 (The Leak):** The margin gap is primarily driven by **Operations/SG&A costs**, which are dragging our margin down by a massive **152 bps**.
*   **Finding 3 (The Strength):** We have the most productive workforce in the industry. We generate **₹48.54 Lakhs** in revenue per employee (Highest in the peer group).

---

## Slide 4: Strategic Recommendations
**Headline:** Three actionable steps to boost our margins.

1.  **Streamline Operations (SG&A):**
    *   *Action:* Implement Zero-Based Budgeting for all administrative and facility costs.
    *   *Impact:* Target to recover the 152 bps gap over 2 years.
2.  **Optimize Employee Cost (5% Cut):**
    *   *Action:* Reduce subcontracting and improve offshore utilization.
    *   *Impact:* Adds **288 bps** to our margin, pushing us above 20%.
3.  **Leverage Productivity for High-Margin Deals:**
    *   *Action:* Since our teams are highly productive, we should stop chasing low-margin revenue and negotiate value-based pricing.
    *   *Impact:* Protects our 23.3% growth while improving bottom-line profit.

---

## Slide 5: Next Steps & Implementation
**Headline:** Pilot, Measure, and Scale.

*   **Immediate Action (Next 30 Days):** Run a 4-week pilot in the Delivery department to identify SG&A waste and test the 5% cost reduction strategy.
*   **Tracking:** Use the newly built Power BI dashboard to monitor the Margin Bridge and SG&A ratios on a monthly basis.
*   **Financial Goal:** Increase EBITDA margin from **17.24% to 19.5% - 20% by end of FY27**, generating an additional ₹500+ Cr in EBITDA.
